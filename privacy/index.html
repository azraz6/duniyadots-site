Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

DuniyaDots is a private prayer-tracking app. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information, and it is deliberately short because very little happens.

The short version

DuniyaDots does not have a server, an account system, or any analytics. Your prayer history, your reflections and your location stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, sold, or shared.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage (and, for widget data, in an App Group container shared with the DuniyaDots widget extension). None of it is transmitted anywhere.

DataWhyWhere it lives
Prayer records (which prayers you marked, for which dates, and the time you marked them) This is the app's entire purpose — showing your consistency over time On-device database (SwiftData)
Daily reflections you choose to write To show your note back to you on that day On-device database (SwiftData)
Your location — coordinates, or a city you selected manually To calculate prayer times for where you are On-device preferences
Your settings (calculation method, Asr method, time adjustments, reminder choices, theme) To keep the app configured the way you set it On-device preferences
Whether you completed onboarding and granted notification permission To avoid asking twice On-device preferences

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase everything from within the app using Settings › Reset all data, which removes every record immediately.

Location

Location is used for one purpose only: calculating local prayer times on your device.

  • Location is optional. If you decline, you can choose a city by hand and every feature still works.
  • The app requests location only when you tap “Use my location” — never on launch, and never in the background.
  • Your coordinates are never sent to us or to anyone else. Prayer times are computed locally by an offline calculation library.
  • We do not access your location while the app is closed.

If you grant location access once, the app remembers the last coordinates it received so it can keep working offline. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Photo library (wallpaper)

If you choose a photo to place behind your wallpaper artwork, iOS shows you a picker that runs outside the app. DuniyaDots receives only the single image you select — it has no access to the rest of your library, and no photo-library permission is requested. The image is composited into your wallpaper on your device and is never uploaded. The app does not write to your photo library; saving an export uses the standard iOS share sheet, which you control.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. There is no push server and no remote notification infrastructure. If you decline notifications, nothing is scheduled.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect any personal identifiers.
  • We do not collect analytics, crash telemetry, or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites (the app has no tracking domains).
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we never receive it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Data collection declaration

In accordance with Apple's App Privacy requirements, DuniyaDots declares: Data Not Collected. The app collects no data of any kind from this app. All processing happens on-device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of DuniyaDots adds a feature that changes any of the above — for example, optional iCloud sync — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

DuniyaDots is a personal record of your own prayers. It is a tracker, not a religious authority.
