Support

DuniyaDots is a quiet app with no account and no server, so there is nothing to sign into and nothing to recover. Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, email us.

Prayer times

How are prayer times calculated?

Times are calculated on your device from your coordinates and the calculation method you choose in Settings › Calculation. Nothing is downloaded, and the app works fully offline.

Which calculation method should I choose?

Pick whichever is used by the mosque or authority nearest you. The six available are Muslim World League, ISNA (North America), Umm al-Qura, Egyptian General Authority, University of Karachi, and Moonsighting Committee.

There is no universally correct answer — the methods differ in the twilight angle they use for Fajr and Isha. Following your local mosque is the most reliable rule.

The times are a minute or two off my local mosque

Set the method first, then fine-tune. Settings › Time adjustments lets you nudge each prayer individually in minutes — for example Fajr +2 or Isha −3.

The Asr time looks early or late

Asr depends on the juristic method. The Shafi'i method is earlier; the Hanafi method is later. Switch between them in Settings › Calculation.

Summer times look odd where I live

At high latitudes the sun may not dip far enough below the horizon for a normal Fajr or Isha, so a high-latitude rule is applied. You can change which one is used in Settings › Calculation.

Can I use the app without granting location access?

Yes, and it is a first-class option rather than a degraded one. Choose a city by hand in Settings › Location, or from the prompt on the Today screen. Prayer times are then calculated from that city.

I granted location access by mistake. Can I take it back?

Yes — iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. The app keeps working from whatever city you set by hand. Revoking access never locks you out.

Tracking and history

Why does a past day say “not logged”?

The app never assumes you missed a prayer. A day stays blank until you log it yourself, and you can fill in earlier days from History at any time. Nothing is silently marked against you.

What do the dot states mean?

An outlined dot is a prayer you haven't logged yet. A filled dot is one you marked as prayed. You can tap any dot to change it, on today or on any past day.

Can I fill in a day I missed?

Yes. Open History, tap the day, and mark the prayers you prayed. You can also narrow the matrix to a single prayer, such as Fajr alone, to see the shape of one habit.

Changing my location changed my history

It doesn't. Your prayer records are independent of location and calculation method — only future prayer times change. Historical records are never recalculated or removed.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders

Two things to check, in order:

  • Settings › Reminders in the app — the Prayer notifications switch needs to be on, and so does the individual prayer you are expecting.
  • iOS Settings › Notifications › DuniyaDots — notifications must be allowed. If you tapped “Not now” during onboarding, this is where to turn them on.
Can reminders arrive before the prayer time?

Yes. Under Settings › Reminders, set Remind me to At prayer time, or 5, 10 or 15 minutes before.

Do reminders need an internet connection?

No. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. There is no push server, and nothing about your reminders leaves the device.

Widgets

Which widgets are available?

Home Screen small and medium widgets, and Lock Screen circular and rectangular widgets. The small widget shows today's five dots and your count; the medium widget lists the prayers with their times and the next prayer.

Can I mark a prayer from the widget?

Yes. Tap a prayer in the Home Screen widget and it is marked as prayed immediately, without opening the app. It writes to the same data the app uses, so the app reflects it next time you open it.

The widget looks out of date

Widgets refresh on the system's schedule, not continuously, so a change can take a short while to appear. Marking a prayer from the widget itself updates it immediately. Removing and re-adding the widget also forces a rebuild.

Wallpaper

The dots sit underneath my clock

Turn on Show the safe area in the Wallpaper screen. It marks the strips iOS reserves for the status bar, clock and widgets, the buttons, and the dock, so you can place your dots where they will actually be seen. The guide is only a preview aid — it is never part of the exported image.

Can I put the dots over my own photo?

Yes. Choose Photo as the background, pick an image, then use Dim the photo and Soften the photo until the dots read clearly. The photo is composited on your device and is never uploaded.

Is the wallpaper a live background that keeps updating?

No, and this is worth being clear about. The wallpaper is an image you export and set yourself. It shows your dots as they were when you made it. iOS does not allow an app to keep rewriting your wallpaper, so re-export from the Wallpaper screen whenever you want it refreshed.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. There is no account, no server and no analytics, so nothing you log is ever uploaded. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Can I move my history to a new phone?

Not yet. Prayer history lives in the app's on-device database and the app has no export or sync, so it does not transfer between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app. We would rather say this plainly than have you find out later.

How do I erase everything?

Settings › Reset all data removes every prayer record and reflection immediately and cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes all of it.

Requests and bugs

How do I ask for a feature or report a bug?

Email info@qshoppro.com. Please mention your device and iOS version, and describe what you expected and what happened instead.

What about Qibla, a Quran reader, or Ramadan features?

Not in this version, and deliberately so — DuniyaDots is scoped to prayer consistency and reflection. Suggestions are genuinely welcome.

DuniyaDots is a personal record of your own prayers. It is a tracker, not a religious authority. Please confirm prayer times with your local mosque.
