Prayer tracker for iOS

Five prayers. One day at a time.

DuniyaDots tracks your salah the way a quiet journal would — visually, privately, and entirely on your device. Each day is five dots. Fill them as you pray.

Your day in five dots

Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, each with its own time. One tap marks a prayer; tapping again undoes it.

Times calculated on your device

Six calculation methods, Shafi'i or Hanafi Asr, a high-latitude rule, and per-prayer minute adjustments. Nothing is fetched from a server.

A history you can read at a glance

Every day becomes five dots in a matrix. Look back over 30 days, 3 months, a year or all time, and tap any day to fill in what you missed.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising. Your prayer history never leaves your device.

Also included

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, with marking straight from the widget
  • A wallpaper builder that turns your dots into a lock screen or home screen image
  • Gentle per-prayer reminders you control, scheduled locally
  • An optional short reflection on any day, kept private on your device
  • Optional Hijri date display, and a considered light and dark appearance
  • Full Dynamic Type support up to the largest accessibility sizes

What it deliberately is not

No leaderboards, no badges, no streaks to protect, and no messages telling you that you are behind. Consistency is worth building for its own sake. A prayer is never marked as missed unless you say so yourself.
